AURA Call Clarity Checklist (Important)

Please review the checklist below before your call.

01

You may reschedule once, and it must be done at least 24 hours before your appointment time. Any no show or last minute cancellation will require a 3-month wait before reapplying.

02

You will receive a Zoom link before the call. Please test it in advance and ensure you can join without any technical issues.

03

Please be in a quiet, private, and uninterrupted environment for your call. You cannot be driving, walking, working, or in transit. If the environment is not appropriate, the call may be ended and considered a no-show.

04

It is completely normal to feel nervous or uncertain beforehand. Come as you are. This is a safe, confidential space, and our only goal is to support you and help you understand how the program works.

05

The first call determines if now is the right season for you to begin this level of work. Achilles or one of our doctors will let you know if you are ready. We do not accept clients based on financial ability. We accept women who are genuinely prepared to commit to the emotional and neurological work required to transform their patterns.

06

If you miss your appointment or no-show, you will need to wait 3 months before re-applying and scheduling again.
