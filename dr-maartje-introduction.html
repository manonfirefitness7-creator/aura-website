A Message From Our Head of Neuropsychology

Meet Dr. Maartje Hidalgo

Learn what AURA asks of every woman, how neuropsychology coaching creates change, and why your participation is essential to the process.

Experience & Credentials

The clinical depth behind the method

Dr. Maartje brings nearly 20 years of experience as a neuropsychologist. Before creating AURA's neuropsychology coaching approach, she spent a decade treating complex conditions including PTSD, personality disorders, depression, anxiety, and ADHD.

What To Expect

This is active work, not passive conversation

AURA is designed to help you understand your brain, interrupt old patterns, and build tools you can use long after the program ends.

01

Mutual Fit Comes First

The introductory call is a mutual evaluation. A strong fit, readiness, and trust are essential foundations for progress.

02

Learn and Apply

Sessions focus on skills, tools, stories, and practical coaching. The goal is not simply to vent. It is to understand and practice new responses.

03

Use the Support

Direct communication helps you catch old patterns as they happen, so you can respond differently instead of remaining stuck in them.

04

Do the Work Between Sessions

Transformation requires honesty, consistency, practice, and a willingness to use the tools even when the work feels unfamiliar or uncomfortable.

Why The Process Works

100%

Current reported success rate

AURA currently reports a 100% success rate among participating clients. That outcome is supported by careful mutual selection, direct coaching, close support, and women who actively practice what they learn. The program provides the framework and tools; each participant must bring commitment, openness, and consistent action.

Past and current outcomes are not a guarantee of any individual's results. Progress varies and depends in part on participation, consistency, circumstances, and readiness.

The Goal

Become the master of your patterns, not a prisoner of them.

The work is designed to build self knowledge, self worth, emotional flexibility, and a practical toolkit for navigating life's challenges and creating healthier relationships.
