1. Program Overview

A.U.R.A. is a neuropsychology coaching program designed to help individuals reframe their thoughts, build healthier habits, and achieve personal transformation through brain-based strategies and wellness techniques. Our program provides guidance through mental and emotional challenges while integrating physical wellness practices. However, A.U.R.A. is strictly an educational and coaching program; it does not provide medical, psychological, or psychiatric treatment and should not be considered a replacement for professional healthcare services.

By enrolling in the A.U.R.A. program, you confirm that you: • Are at least 18 years of age. • Identify as biologically female. • Are not currently experiencing suicidal thoughts or tendencies and have not been recently hospitalized for severe mental health crises. 4. No-Show & Rescheduling Policy

The A.U.R.A. Program consists of twelve (12) transformative sessions, designed to enhance both mental and physical resilience: Six (6) Neurological Coaching Sessions: These sessions focus on rewiring thought patterns, strengthening cognitive resilience, and enhancing emotional well-being. Six (6) Physical Performance Sessions: Beyond traditional fitness, these sessions are designed to deepen your understanding of how your body responds to movement, helping you build strength, improve body awareness, and align your training with your specific goals. It's not just about working out, it's about mastering the connection between mind and body for long-term success. If you fail to attend a scheduled session without prior notice (no-show), you will forfeit that session, and it will not be rescheduled. If you need to reschedule, you must provide at least 24 hours notice before your session. In the case of a genuine emergency, you may request a reschedule, but documentation of the emergency will be required.
