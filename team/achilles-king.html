Founder

Achilles King

A.U.R.A. was born from real pain. Pain I grew up watching in my own mother.

She carried wounds she never healed and held strength she was never supported in. Losing her made me realize how many women spend their whole lives carrying emotional wounds in silence because they’ve never had the right environment, education, or support to truly understand what’s happening beneath the surface.

That loss pushed me to educate myself deeply in neuropsychology, the nervous system, human behavior, and the patterns that shape how we love, attach, protect ourselves, and make decisions.

But I am not the neuropsychologist, and I believe that distinction matters.

My role is to educate, help women recognize patterns, coordinate their journey, and serve as the bridge between them and the qualified neuropsychology professionals and other experts within the A.U.R.A. ecosystem.

And our approach is different.

We do not do traditional talk therapy.

We focus on helping women understand the patterns their nervous system has learned and why they can continue repeating what is familiar, even when they consciously know they want something different.

And yes, God is at the center of A.U.R.A.

We believe in prayer. We believe in faith. But we also believe God is not going to do our emotional homework for us.

You can pray for a healthier relationship, but prayer alone does not teach you why you keep choosing the same type of person.

You can pray for peace, but you still have to understand what keeps your nervous system living in survival.

You can pray for change, but you still have to participate in that change.

God gave us a brain capable of learning, adapting, and changing. Faith gives us the foundation. The work requires our participation.

That is why A.U.R.A. exists.

To bring faith, neuropsychology, the nervous system, physical wellness, and qualified professionals together so women can stop simply talking about their patterns and begin understanding where they come from and doing the work to change them.
