Privacy Policy

A.U.R.A. Privacy Policy

Effective October 1, 2026. This Privacy Policy explains how A.U.R.A. collects, uses, and shares information when you visit our website, submit an application or contact form, join a list, book an appointment, or otherwise communicate with us.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you choose to provide, including your name, email address, phone number, messages, application responses, and other information submitted through our website or application. If you book an appointment, we also collect information needed to manage that appointment, such as the appointment date and time, timezone, booking status, and meeting details. If you choose to receive text messages, we collect your phone number and your record of SMS consent.

2. How We Use Information

We use this information to respond to inquiries; review applications; determine the appropriate A.U.R.A. call or next step; schedule, confirm, and manage appointments; provide meeting information and requested communications; operate our services; maintain records; support participants; protect our services; and comply with applicable legal obligations. We use SMS consent information and phone numbers for text messaging only when the individual has opted in.

3. SMS Privacy and Choices

SMS consent and phone numbers collected for SMS purposes will not be sold or shared with third parties or affiliates for their marketing or promotional purposes.

We may share information with service providers only as necessary to operate A.U.R.A. services, including providers that support communications and text-message delivery. These providers may use the information only to perform services for A.U.R.A. and not for their own marketing or promotional purposes.

If you opt in to receive text messages from A.U.R.A., you may reply STOP at any time to opt out. Reply HELP for assistance. Opting out of text messages does not prevent us from contacting you through other methods when permitted or necessary to provide a service you requested.

4. When We Share Information

We do not sell personal information. We may share information with service providers that help us operate the website, receive and store submissions, manage applications and appointments, host online meetings, and deliver email or text communications. We may also disclose information when required by law or when reasonably necessary to protect the rights, safety, or security of A.U.R.A., our participants, or others.

5. Data Retention and Security

We retain personal information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the requested services, maintain appropriate business records, resolve disputes, and meet legal obligations. We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards designed to protect the information we maintain. No method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure.

6. Your Choices

You may choose not to provide information, although doing so may prevent us from responding to a request, reviewing an application, or arranging an appointment. You may ask to review, correct, or delete personal information by contacting us at support@aurarewire.com. We will respond as required by applicable law.

7. Adults Only

A.U.R.A. services are intended for adults age 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through our application or booking process.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our practices or legal obligations change. The effective date at the top of this page indicates when the policy was last updated.

9. Contact and Terms

Questions about this Privacy Policy may be sent to support@aurarewire.com. Your use of A.U.R.A. services is also subject to our Terms & Conditions.
